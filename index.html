Help

Safety

Dry training only — on land, lying down. Never train breath holds in or near water, in a bath, or while standing or driving. A blackout comes without warning.

Only one maximum attempt per session. The PB button rests for 10 minutes after one.

Don't hyperventilate before a hold: calm, slow breaths.

How this works

CO₂ table — the hold stays the same (half your PB), the rest between holds gets 15 seconds shorter each round. Trains you to cope with the urge to breathe.

O₂ table — the rest stays at 2 minutes, the hold gets longer each round, up to 80 % of your PB. Trains your body to manage with less oxygen.

PB attempt — a calm breathe-up, then one hold for as long as you like. Beat your PB and the screen turns gold and your PB updates itself.

During a hold — tap anywhere on the screen each time you feel a contraction. Each tap adds a white dot.

To stop — press and hold anywhere for one second. In a PB attempt that ends the hold and saves it.

Colours — green: breathe · amber: get ready (last 10 s) · blue: hold · gold: past your PB.

Sound — the voice says "Breathe" and "Hold", counts down the last seconds with beeps, so you can keep your eyes closed. Keep the screen on — the app keeps it awake by itself.

Before a table you can change the number of rounds and the hold length with − and +. It remembers your choice.
